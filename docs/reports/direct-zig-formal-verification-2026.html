The Three Takeaways That Matter Most

  1. Close the "Model Drift" Gap First: Today Guillotine has 32 Bitwuzla/Z3 SMT proofs and 43 Lean 4 theorems, but almost all of them verify hand-transcribed models—not the actual Zig code. Only 22 tiny acyclic functions in src/core/verified_ops.zig are verified directly from Zig's emitted LLVM IR. Moving those proofs onto Zean-extracted Zig AIR eliminates human transcription bugs immediately.
  2. Refactor for Zero-Cost Verifiability (Don't Fight preserve_none): Guillotine's biggest speed trick—preprocessed dispatch schedules with preserve_none indirect tail-call chains—is kryptonite for every formal verifier on earth. The fix is clean and zero-overhead: split every opcode and synthetic handler into a pure, inline, Zean-admissible semantics kernel + a 5-line preserve_none trampoline. -OReleaseFast emits identical assembly while making the entire EVM directly verifiable.
  3. Combine Source-Level Proofs (Zean → Lean 4) with Binary-Adjacent Proofs (LLVM 20 IR + Alive2): AIR proofs verify unbounded loops, slices, dispatch compilation, and journal rollback against GuillotineSpec.Evm, but still trust LLVM. Pairing Zean with our expanded LLVM 20 IR verifier + Alive2 + bounded panic-freedom checks closes both the source-to-spec gap and the compiler-miscompilation gap—and AI proof agents checked by Lean's kernel can keep proofs green automatically on every PR.

1 What You Saw on Zig Forums (and Got DM'd About)

Zig has no built-in formal verifier in ziglang/zig (unlike SPARK/Ada or Dafny). Instead, two concrete efforts have dominated Ziggit, Reddit, and Hacker News formal-verification threads through 2025–2026:

1. Zean (okcontract/zean)

Exact Fit for Guillotine

Built by OKcontract Labs and spun out of formally verifying oksolc (a Solidity compiler written in Zig!). Featured on Ziggit and Show HN: Zean Transpiles Zig to Lean.

  • How it works: Uses an instrumented Zig 0.17.1 compiler to capture functions from Zig's AIR (after comptime & type-checking, before LLVM lowering) and emits executable Lean 4 (v4.35.0) definitions.
  • Clean proof separation: Generated Lean code stays separate from handwritten specs, loop invariants, and proofs; zean --check fails CI whenever Zig source drifts out of sync.
  • Memory & control flow: Uses Lean 4's Std.WP / air_vcgen to generate obligations for slices, word/byte buffers, pointer permissions, initialization, arithmetic bounds, loops, direct/mutual recursion, and release/acquire atomics.
  • Honest limits: Currently v0.2.x with strict admission rules—rejects unsupported AIR ops, opaque pointers (*anyopaque), indirect function-pointer tail calls, and partial struct layouts. Also trusts the LLVM backend below AIR.

2. Zig AIR → Boogie & Comptime Contracts

Early / Experimental

The other recurring Ziggit thread explores extending Zig's AIR printer to emit Boogie IVL (Microsoft Research's intermediate verification language backed by Z3), using inlined "ghost functions" as contracts and assertions.

  • Appeal: SMT-first automation (similar in spirit to Dafny or Verus)—less manual proof writing for local assertions.
  • Why NOT our primary bet: Still experimental prototype code with no maintained CLI or memory model for real codebases, and zero Ethereum/EVM formalization ecosystem.
  • Contrast with Lean 4: Ethereum's entire formal verification momentum in 2026 lives in Lean 4—including Guillotine's own GuillotineSpec, Nethermind's EvmYulLean (99.99% Cancun spec compliance), Paradigm's Solidus, Verity, and EF's leanVM.
Why Zig Is Secretly Easier to Formally Verify Than Rust or C++

Two Zig design choices are massive tailwinds for direct verification: (1) comptime resolves before AIR—so when Zig compiles Evm(.{ .hardfork = .OSAKA, .enable_fusion = true }), every generic, hardfork flag, and table lookup is already monomorphized into flat, concrete imperative code! (2) No borrow-checker / trait-solver / hidden-control-flow explosion—unlike rocq-of-rust, you don't have to model Rust's complex trait resolution or panic unwinding; errors are plain tagged return values (!T) and allocations are explicit.

The 3 Highest-Risk Unverified Failure Modes in Guillotine

  • 1. Dispatch Schedule & _unsafe Pre-Validation Soundness: Guillotine skips runtime stack bounds checks inside handlers (push_unsafe, binary_op_unsafe) because basic-block metadata pre-validates stack depth at block entry. If any block boundary (frame entry, JUMPDEST, untaken JUMPI fallthrough, or fused synthetic op) miscomputes min_stack or max_stack, it causes out-of-bounds memory corruption in ReleaseFast!
  • 2. Register-Pinned sp / gas Flush Discipline: Under handler_abi.zig, sp and gas live in registers across preserve_none tail calls while frame.stack.stack_ptr and frame.gas_remaining are stale. A single forgotten flush() before a host call or exit causes silent consensus divergence.
  • 3. Journal Rollback & Hardfork Edge Cases: Journal.revert_to_snapshot uses a prefix_max O(1) tail-truncation fast path falling back to full scan, coupled with original_storage caching for EIP-2200/3529 SSTORE metering, EIP-2929 warm/cold lists, and EIP-1153 transient storage. Subtle nested-revert bugs are classic EVM consensus killers.

4 The Best & Most Ambitious Way: 4-Pillar Direct Verification

To surpass revm's formal verification while keeping Guillotine's blazing preserve_none dispatch speed, we should build a dual-track direct verification pipeline anchored in Lean 4 + LLVM 20 IR. Every proof reads directly from compiler IR—never a hand-copied model.

Figure 1: End-to-End Direct Zig Verification Architecture — closing both the source-to-spec gap (via Zean AIR → Lean 4) and the compiler/UB gap (via LLVM 20 IR + Alive2).

Pillar 1: Zero-Cost "Verifiable Core + Thin preserve_none Shell"

Trying to push callconv(.aarch64_preserve_none) indirect tail-call chains through a formal verifier is a trap. Instead, every opcode handler, fusion handler, and bytecode analysis pass splits cleanly into an inline pure kernel and a 5-line dispatch trampoline:

Verifiable Core (Zean AIR + LLVM IR target) 100% Provable 
// Pure, zero-cost, Zean-admissible Zig kernel
// No *anyopaque, no function pointers, no tracer
pub inline fn execPushAdd(
    stack: []u256,
    sp_idx: *usize,
    imm: u256,
) void {
    // Precondition proven by BasicBlock guard:
    // sp_idx.* < stack.len (at least 1 item on stack)
    stack[sp_idx.*] = imm +% stack[sp_idx.*];
}
Thin Dispatch Shell (handlers_*_synthetic.zig) Zero Overhead 
// Compiles to the EXACT same machine code!
pub fn push_add_inline(
    self: *FrameType,
    cursor: [*]const Dispatch.Item,
    sp: [*]WordType,
    gas: FrameType.GasType,
) callconv(handler_abi.handler_cc) ExitCode {
    N.before(self, .PUSH_ADD_INLINE, cursor, sp, gas);
    const imm = cursor[1].push_inline.value;
    kernels.execPushAdd(self.stackSlice(), &idx, imm);
    return N.next(self, .PUSH_ADD_INLINE, cursor, sp, gas);
}

Notice the magic: we already started this pattern in src/core/verified_ops.zig! Scaling this exact pattern across src/instructions/, src/bytecode/, src/preprocessor/, and src/storage/journal.zig lets Zean extract the real implementation directly from Zig AIR while LLVM inlines the kernel straight back into the preserve_none register-pinned hot loop.

Pillar 2: Direct Zig AIR → Lean 4 via Zean (and Extending Zean Upstream)

Zean already speaks Zig 0.17.1 and emits Lean 4 (`v4.35.0`). Adopting it gives us two immediate breakthroughs:

  • Kill Hand-Transcribed Models Forever: Today verify/lean/GuillotineVerify/ manually rewrites signextendFast, blockStackGuardFast, checkMemoryBoundsFast, etc. in Lean. With Zean, those Lean definitions are generated directly from Guillotine's actual Zig AIR, and our existing 43 Lean theorems prove properties about the real Zig source.
  • Unbounded Loops & Stateful Data Structures: Unlike SMT-only LLVM checks (which reject loops), Zean + Lean 4's Std.Do / air_vcgen verifies arbitrary loops via loop invariants—unlocking direct proofs of bytecode_analyze.zig, JumpTable binary search, Journal.revert_to_snapshot, RLP decoding, and FastLZ decompression.
  • Strategic Partnership / Upstream Extension: Zean is v0.2.x and built by Ethereum engineers (OKcontract Labs, for oksolc). To verify 100% of Guillotine's core, we should track and contribute 3 specific AIR profile extensions to Zean: (a) first-class u256 / i256 → Lean BitVec 256 lowering, (b) tagged unions (for Dispatch.Item & JournalEntry), and (c) Zig error unions (!T → Lean Except).

Pillar 3: Direct LLVM 20 IR, Alive2 & Panic-Unreachability (Closing the Compiler Gap)

Formal Land's revm proofs (in Rocq) and Zean (on AIR) both stop before LLVM optimization. Guillotine already has a huge head start here with verify/llvm/llvm_ir_verify.py! We push this much further than any other EVM:

  • Expand verify/llvm/llvm_ir_verify.py: Grow from 22 ref/fast pairs to every pure opcode kernel, gas calculator, memory/stack guard, and synthetic fusion, verifying bit-exact equivalence and zero Alive2-style poison/UB directly on -OReleaseFast LLVM 20 IR.
  • Compiler Miscompilation Guard with Alive2: Run Alive2 comparing Zig's unoptimized (-ODebug/-OReleaseSafe) LLVM IR against -OReleaseFast LLVM IR for critical kernels—catching LLVM backend miscompilations before they hit mainnet.
  • Automated Panic-Freedom Gate: Export verifiable kernels under -OReleaseSafe (where every Zig overflow, slice bounds check, and @intCast lowers to a trap/__zig_panic) and prove __zig_panic is mathematically unreachable under verified preconditions using Crux-LLVM / SeaHorn (or -ofmt=c + CBMC for bounded routines).
  • Track lean-mlir / Trivet (Late 2026): For the ultimate no-trust-gap proof on critical arithmetic & crypto glue, lift LLVM SSA IR straight into Lean 4 with bv_decide so even the LLVM-to-SMT translator leaves the Trusted Computing Base!

Pillar 4: The 2026 Unfair Advantage — AI Proof Factory Checked by Lean's Kernel

Historically, verifying an EVM required a team of 5–10 formal-methods PhDs writing 50,000+ lines of Coq/Rocq by hand. Three 2026 breakthroughs flip the economics completely:

  • bv_decide: Automatically bit-blasts 256-bit EVM goals to CaDiCal and checks the LRAT UNSAT certificate inside Lean's kernel.
  • grind: Native SMT-inspired congruence closure + linear integer arithmetic (cutsat) + algebraic reasoning built directly into Lean 4—cutting boilerplate by 5–10×.
  • Zero-Trust AI Proof Synthesis: Using Smithers workflows, AI agents can read a changed Zig function, run zean, inspect the open air_vcgen goals, and iterate until Lean compiles with zero sorry and zero custom axioms (enforced by #print axioms in CI). An LLM can hallucinate code; it can never fake a Lean kernel check.
